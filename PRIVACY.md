# nosave privacy policy

nosave collects no data.

- It runs only on Google search result pages and only reads the buttons on the page, to
  find and hide the Save button shown over pictures.
- It stores nothing: no settings, history or identifiers, locally or anywhere else.
- It makes no network requests and has no server, account, analytics or third-party code.
- It requests no browser permissions.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/Morass/nosave/issues.
